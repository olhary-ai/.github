[Olhary](https://olhary.ai) helps you research Brazil's public tenders, investigate links between companies and people, and manage legal cases with AI.

## What you can do

- Find tenders, compare competing companies and check reference prices.
- Trace links between companies, their partners, public contracts and sanctions.
- Organize cases, deadlines and documents. Prepare legal drafts for your team to review.

## Connect your AI tools

Search public tenders, check company sanctions and find procurement catalog codes from Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT or Cursor. See [plugins and setup steps](https://github.com/olhary-ai/plugins).
